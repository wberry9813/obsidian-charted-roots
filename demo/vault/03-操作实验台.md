## A. 一键生成所有 Bases

然后打开 People / Places / Events / Organizations / Sources / Universes / Notes / Research Base。

- Multiple marriages
- DNA Matches
- Needs research
- Missing parents
- By collection
- By research level

接着执行参考编号后再看：

- Ahnentafel ordered
- d'Aboville ordered
- By lineage
- By generation
